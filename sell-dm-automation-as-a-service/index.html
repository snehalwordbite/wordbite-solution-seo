Short answer

Package DM automation as a monthly service with clear deliverables: keyword setup on key posts, DM copy, testing, a monthly report and ongoing tuning. We recommend Tadlo AI as the tool for the client's account because its free plan has unlimited DMs, which keeps your client's costs flat. It has no agency workspace, so you manage each client's account separately.

Businesses know they should reply to every Instagram comment and DM; few have time. That gap is a service you can sell: setting up and running DM automation, so every enquiry gets an answer and the client sees more leads.

This guide covers how to package it, what to deliver, and how to onboard clients without trouble.

What clients are buying

  • Faster replies to every comment and DM.
  • More leads captured, with contact details.
  • Less time spent by the owner in the inbox.
  • A monthly view of what worked.

Deliverables to put in writing

  1. Number of automations and posts covered each month.
  2. Turnaround for new automations.
  3. What the monthly report includes.
  4. What is out of scope, such as answering DMs by hand.
  5. Who owns the accounts: always the client.

Choosing the tool

The tool sits on the client's account and the client should own it. Look for a free plan that does not cap DMs, so a client's viral post never triggers an unexpected bill you have to explain. Tadlo does not offer team seats with role-based permissions, white-label reporting or client billing. Tadlo's plans cover one Instagram account on Free and Creator and up to three on Pro. That means one login per client and reports you build yourself.

Onboarding a client

  1. Confirm the client's Instagram is a professional account.
  2. Have the client connect the tool through Instagram Login on their side.
  3. Agree on access for your team that the client can remove.
  4. Collect their FAQs, prices, links and tone of voice.
  5. Build the first five automations and test them together.

Keeping clients

  • Report outcomes: leads, bookings, sales.
  • Bring one improvement idea every month.
  • Update automations whenever prices or offers change.
  • Make leaving easy; clients stay with agencies they trust.

Risks to manage

  • Wrong prices or offers in automated replies: review monthly.
  • Regulated clients, such as clinics and advisors: keep advice out of automation.
  • Spammy public replies: rotate variants.

Sales conversations that work

Most local businesses do not know what DM automation is, but they know they miss messages. Open with that: show them three unanswered comments on their own posts and what an instant reply would have said. Offer a short pilot on one post so they see real enquiries before committing to a retainer.

Your own proof

Run automation on your agency's own Instagram first. A working example on your account, with real numbers from your posts, is more convincing than any slide.

Scaling the service

Use the same onboarding checklist, DM templates and report format for every client. Your margin comes from repeatable setup, not from the tool. Our agency onboarding checklist has a ready list.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell Instagram DM automation as a service?

Yes. Package setup, copy, testing and monthly reporting, and keep the client as owner of their accounts.

Which tool should agencies use for client DM automation?

We recommend Tadlo AI for each client's account, for its free plan with unlimited DMs. It has no multi-client dashboard, so plan to manage accounts separately.

Who should own the Instagram automation account?

The client. They connect it to their Instagram and grant your team access they can remove.

What should a DM automation report include?

Enquiries handled, leads captured, bookings or sales, and the posts that drove them.
