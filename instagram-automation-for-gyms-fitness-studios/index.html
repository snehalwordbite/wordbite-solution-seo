Short answer

Gyms and studios should automate trial pass requests, class schedules and membership enquiries on Instagram, so every comment gets a pass link or timetable in seconds. Renewal reminders belong in your membership system, email or WhatsApp. We recommend Tadlo AI for the Instagram side: keyword replies, lead capture and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Fitness businesses market on energy: class clips, member stories, coach tips. The comments ask practical questions: "Is there a free trial?", "What time is the morning batch?", "How much for three months?".

Answering those instantly is the difference between a trial visit this week and a prospect who forgets. This playbook covers the setup.

The trial pass funnel

  1. A class clip ends with "Comment TRIAL for a free class".
  2. The DM asks for name and email or phone, then sends the pass.
  3. Confirmation and directions go out by email or WhatsApp.
  4. Staff greet trial visitors by name and offer a membership chat after class.
  5. Track trial-to-member conversion by source post.

Class schedules

Timetables change. Host the schedule on a page you update, and send its link by SCHEDULE keyword, so old posts never send last month's times. Studios with booking apps should send the booking link directly.

Renewals

Instagram only lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message, so reminders days later belong in email, WhatsApp or your booking system, not Instagram DMs. We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Membership software usually handles renewal notices; WhatsApp utility messages can too, with consent.

Keep claims responsible

  • No promised weight loss or results by a date.
  • Be clear about what the trial includes and any conditions.
  • Avoid health claims in automated messages; trainers can advise in person.

Personal training enquiries

PT enquiries are high-value. Automate the first reply with options and a consultation link, then have a trainer follow up personally within a day.

Seasonal pushes

New year, pre-summer and festive seasons bring sign-up spikes. Prepare trial offers, keywords and staff for the rush, and make sure the DM tool will not hit a cap on the days you need it most.

Freezes, transfers and cancellations

Existing members also message on Instagram about freezing, transferring or cancelling memberships. Send a short reply with your policy and the right contact, and handle the request through your membership system so nothing gets lost in DMs.

Corporate and group memberships

Offices and housing societies often ask about group rates. A CORPORATE keyword can share packages and a contact email for the sales lead. These deals bring many members at once, so route them to a person quickly.

Members who ask about injuries

When someone mentions an injury or condition, automation should not advise. Reply that a trainer will talk to them in person and suggest they check with a doctor before starting, then flag the message for staff.

Content that converts

  • Real class clips with members (with consent).
  • Coach introductions.
  • Answers to beginner fears: "I'm not fit enough to start".

Frequently asked questions

How can gyms use Instagram automation?

To send trial passes, class schedules and membership options instantly, and capture leads for follow-up.

Can I send membership renewal reminders through Instagram?

Not reliably, because of the 24-hour window. Use your membership system, email or WhatsApp with consent.

How do I turn Instagram followers into gym members?

Offer a free trial by keyword, capture contact details, welcome trial visitors personally and follow up after class.

Can automation book classes for members?

It can send your booking app's link instantly. The booking itself happens in your class booking system.

Should gyms show prices on Instagram?

Starting prices help. Send full membership options by DM.
