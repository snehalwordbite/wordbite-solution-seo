Short answer

Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a WhatsApp chat with your business, and Meta's free entry point window means messages in that chat are free for up to 7 days. Reply fast, qualify, and move to a booking or sale within the window. We recommend Tadlo AI for handling the chats: a shared inbox, automated replies and no markup on Meta's charges; we did not find ad-specific features such as ad attribution in its published plans.

A Click-to-WhatsApp ad is a Facebook or Instagram ad whose button opens WhatsApp instead of a website. For service businesses and D2C brands whose customers prefer to chat before buying, it often converts better than a landing page.

This guide covers how the ads work, the free window, and what to do with the conversations they start.

How the free window works

When someone messages you from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad, a free entry point window opens for up to 7 days, during which Meta does not charge for messages. The window applies on Android and iOS. Use it: everything you send in that period, replies, offers and follow-ups, costs nothing in Meta's message charges.

What happens when someone taps the ad

  1. WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message to your business.
  2. The person sends it, starting the conversation.
  3. Your inbox receives the chat like any other.
  4. The free entry point window opens.

Reply in seconds

People who tap an ad expect an answer while they still have WhatsApp open. An automated first reply that answers the ad's offer and asks one question keeps them in the conversation until a person picks it up.

A first reply that works

Ad chat first reply

Hi! Thanks for your interest in {{offer}}. {{one_line_answer}}
What would you like to do next: book, see prices, or ask a question?

Qualify, then hand off

  • Ask one question that sorts leads: service, location or budget.
  • Route serious enquiries to a person within business hours.
  • Send a booking or payment link when they are ready.

Follow up within the window

Because messages are free during the window, a short follow-up a day or two later costs nothing in Meta charges. Send it by hand or as a small broadcast to that day's ad chats. Reminders and follow-up sequences run from the business's own booking or CRM system; we did not find scheduled reminders or sequences in Tadlo's published plans.

Collect opt-in for later

After the window, marketing messages cost the normal rate and need opt-in. Ask during the conversation whether they would like offers on WhatsApp, and record the answer. Meta requires businesses to collect opt-in that clearly states the person is agreeing to receive messages from the business, and names the business.

Instagram ad chats

Click-to-Direct ads open an Instagram DM instead. Instagram lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message (up to 7 days after a click-to-Direct ad), and a person on your team can reply within a separate 7-day human agent window. The same rules apply: reply fast and qualify early.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Click-to-WhatsApp ad?

A Facebook or Instagram ad with a button that opens a WhatsApp chat with your business.

Are messages from Click-to-WhatsApp ads free?

When someone messages you from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad, a free entry point window opens for up to 7 days, during which Meta does not charge for messages.

What should I reply to an ad chat?

Answer the ad's offer in one line and ask one question, within seconds.

Should the first reply be automated?

Yes for the first few seconds, so the person gets an answer while WhatsApp is still open; a person should take over for anything detailed.

Should the first reply include prices?

If the ad mentions a price or offer, repeat it so the person knows they are in the right place; otherwise ask one question first and share prices once you know what they need.

Can I message ad leads after 7 days?

Only within normal WhatsApp rules: service replies when they message you, or marketing templates if they opted in, at normal rates.
