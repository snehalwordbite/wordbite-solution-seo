Short answer

Event organisers should automate ticket links, line-up and venue answers, and early-bird waitlists on Instagram, so every "tickets?" comment converts in seconds. Event-day reminders belong in email or WhatsApp. We recommend Tadlo AI for the Instagram side: keyword replies and email capture with unlimited DMs, which matters on on-sale day.

Events are sold on hype, and hype lives on Instagram. A line-up announcement or a teaser reel brings a wave of comments asking for tickets, prices, dates and age limits, all at once.

This playbook covers how organisers turn that wave into ticket sales without drowning the team.

The early-bird list

  1. Tease the event with "Comment EARLY for first access".
  2. The DM confirms and asks for an email or WhatsApp opt-in.
  3. On on-sale day, email or WhatsApp the list before posting publicly.
  4. Then post the on-sale reel with a TICKETS keyword for everyone else.

Why not remind people on Instagram

Instagram only lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message, so a reminder a week later will not reach most people who commented. Collect emails or WhatsApp opt-ins at the waitlist stage, and send on-sale and event-day reminders there. We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

On-sale day volume

On-sale announcements can bring thousands of comments in an hour. A tool with no monthly DM cap that queues within Instagram's limits makes sure every fan gets the ticket link, even if the last few wait a little longer.

Venue and entry questions

  • Age limits and ID requirements.
  • Bag policy and what is not allowed.
  • Parking, transport and nearest stations.
  • Accessibility information.

Updates and changes

If timings, line-up or venue change, update the automated replies first, then post the change. An automated DM with old details causes more confusion than silence.

Ticket honesty

  • Show the full price including fees.
  • Be clear about refund and transfer policies.
  • Do not use fake scarcity; say "limited" only when it is.

Measure

Track waitlist sign-ups, ticket-link clicks and tickets sold by post. The posts that drive sales tell you what to tease for the next event.

Weather and safety updates

Outdoor events face weather changes. If plans change, update the automated replies first, then post the update in stories and send it to registered attendees by email or WhatsApp. Consistent messages across channels prevent confusion on the day.

Accessibility and inclusion

Visitors with disabilities need specific information: step-free access, seating, assistance and quiet areas. An ACCESS keyword with clear, accurate details shows you have planned for everyone, and saves those visitors from having to ask in public comments.

Sponsors and vendors

Sponsors, vendors and performers also reach out on Instagram. A PARTNER keyword that sends your sponsorship deck and contact email keeps those conversations separate from ticket buyers and gets them to the right person.

Lost and found, and event-day questions

During and after the event, DMs spike with practical questions: lost items, re-entry, timings. Prepare short keyword replies for these, and keep a person on the inbox during the event itself.

After the event

Share highlights with a keyword that sends photos or a feedback form, and invite people to join the list for the next event.

Frequently asked questions

How can event organisers use Instagram automation?

To send ticket links, line-up and venue details instantly, and build an early-bird list by keyword.

Can I send event reminders through Instagram?

Not reliably, because of Instagram's 24-hour window. Use email or WhatsApp with consent.

How do I handle on-sale day comment spikes?

Use a tool without a monthly DM cap that queues sends within Instagram's limits, and have the ticket link ready before posting.

How do I stop fake ticket sellers in my comments?

Post the official ticket link only through your own replies and DMs, warn followers about resellers, and report fake accounts.

Should I show ticket prices on Instagram?

Yes, including fees. Send tier details and the link by DM.
