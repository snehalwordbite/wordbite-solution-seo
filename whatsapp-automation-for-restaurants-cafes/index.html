Short answer

Restaurants and cafes use WhatsApp to send menus instantly, take table booking enquiries and send offers to opted-in guests. We recommend Tadlo AI for the shared inbox, automated menu replies and broadcasts with cost preview and no markup on Meta's charges. Booking confirmations and reminders run from your reservation system.

Guests message restaurants for the menu, a table for six on Saturday, or whether the kitchen is still open. Staff are busy serving, so the answer often comes too late.

An automated first reply with the menu and booking options handles most of it, and the team picks up the rest when service allows.

The menu reply

Menu reply

Hi {{name}}! Here's our menu: {{menu_link}}.
Reply BOOK to reserve a table, ORDER for takeaway, or HOURS for today's timings.

Table bookings

When a guest replies BOOK, ask for the date, time and party size, and a host confirms in the inbox after checking your reservation system. Reminders on the day come from the reservation system if it supports them; we did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Takeaway and delivery orders

Small restaurants take takeaway orders on WhatsApp to avoid aggregator commissions. A host confirms the order and pickup time by hand; on Tadlo's Pro plan, a payment link can be sent in chat for prepaid orders.

Send to opted-in guests only, at most a few times a month. In India, Meta charges ₹0.8631 per delivered marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message, effective 1 October 2026.

Collecting opt-in

  • A QR code on the table or bill that opens a chat with a clear offer to join.
  • A checkbox on your online booking form.
  • Staff asking at payment, with the answer recorded.

Private dining and events

Birthday parties, corporate lunches and private dining enquiries are worth a manager's personal reply. Route them in the inbox to the person who handles events, and send the package details as a PDF.

Allergies and dietary questions

Do not let automated replies answer allergen questions beyond pointing to your published allergen information. A staff member who can check with the kitchen should reply.

Reviews

To ask guests for reviews, send an occasional opted-in broadcast with your review link, never in exchange for a discount. We did not find triggered review requests in Tadlo's published plans.

Catering and bulk orders

Office lunches, house parties and festive bulk orders arrive on WhatsApp too. Ask for the date, headcount and budget in the first reply, then hand the chat to the person who quotes catering. A clear written quote in the chat avoids disputes on the day.

Late-night and closed-hours replies

Guests message after closing to book for tomorrow. An automated reply that says when you open, shares the menu and takes the booking details means the host can confirm first thing, instead of the guest booking elsewhere overnight.

Several outlets

Chains can use a number per outlet or a shared number with chats assigned by outlet. Either way, the menu and hours replies must match each outlet.

Measure

Track bookings and takeaway orders from WhatsApp, and opt-outs after broadcasts. If opt-outs rise, send less often.

Frequently asked questions

How do restaurants use WhatsApp automation?

To send menus instantly, take table booking and takeaway enquiries, and send offers to opted-in guests.

Can Tadlo AI confirm table bookings automatically?

A host confirms bookings in the inbox after checking your reservation system. We did not find reservation integrations or scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Can restaurants take orders on WhatsApp?

Yes, with staff confirming each order. Tadlo's Pro plan adds payment links in chat.

How often should a restaurant send offers?

A few times a month at most, to guests who opted in.
