Short answer

B2B and SaaS companies should use Instagram automation to deliver resources by keyword, capture work emails, ask one or two qualifying questions, and send demo booking links. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: keyword DMs, email capture into a leads inbox with CSV export, and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Instagram is not the first channel most B2B teams think of, but founders, product demos and educational reels reach decision-makers too, especially in small and mid-sized businesses. The trick is turning attention into a qualified conversation without a long form.

This playbook covers a light lead qualification setup that suits Instagram.

Light qualification

Ask one or two questions that help sales prioritise: company size, role, or what they want to solve. More than two in a DM loses people; capture the rest on the demo booking form.

Work emails

Ask for a work email when delivering a guide, and say why: "so we can send the guide and the follow-up webinar." Tag the lead with the post and topic, and export to your CRM.

A sample demo DM

Demo request DM

Thanks for your interest in {{product}}! What's your team size? Reply with a number, and here's the demo booking link: {{demo_link}}

Hand off to sales

  1. Export leads with source, topic and answers daily.
  2. Import into your CRM and assign an owner.
  3. Sales replies by email within a working day.
  4. Mark Instagram as the lead source for reporting.

Content that brings B2B leads

  • Founder or product-lead explainers.
  • Short product walkthroughs with a DEMO keyword.
  • Data or insight posts with a GUIDE keyword.
  • Customer stories, with permission.

Respect the channel

Instagram is personal. Keep DMs short and human, avoid jargon, and never add people to a sales sequence without saying so. A friendly first reply gets more demos than a corporate one.

Hiring and employer brand

B2B companies often get as many hiring questions as sales questions on Instagram. A JOBS keyword that sends your careers page keeps candidates out of the sales inbox and makes your team look organised to both audiences. It also stops sales from losing time on messages that were never leads.

Partnership and reseller enquiries

Agencies and resellers sometimes approach through Instagram. A PARTNER keyword that sends your partner programme page and contact email gets them to the right team, and keeps your demo pipeline clean for direct buyers.

Founder-led accounts

Many B2B companies get more traction from a founder's personal professional account than from the company page, because people follow people. The same automation works there: a DEMO or GUIDE keyword on the founder's posts, with leads flowing into the company's inbox and CRM.

Events and webinars

Webinars and conference appearances are natural Instagram moments. A WEBINAR keyword can send the registration link and capture a work email; reminders and recordings then go out by email, where B2B buyers expect them.

When Instagram is the wrong channel

If your buyers are large enterprises with long procurement, Instagram is better for brand and hiring than for leads. Use it for awareness, and keep lead capture light.

Frequently asked questions

Can B2B companies get leads from Instagram?

Yes, especially small and mid-sized buyers. Deliver resources by keyword, capture work emails and send demo links.

How many qualifying questions should I ask in a DM?

One or two. Capture the rest on the demo booking form.

How do I get Instagram leads into my CRM?

Export leads with their source and answers, then import them and assign an owner.

Should I ask for a phone number for demos?

Usually not on Instagram. A work email and team size are enough; the demo booking form can collect the rest.
