Short answer

Travel agencies should automate package enquiries and sample itinerary delivery on Instagram, and capture each traveller's dates, group size and budget for the sales team. Payment reminders belong in email or WhatsApp. We recommend Tadlo AI for the Instagram side: keyword replies, lead capture and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Travel agencies sell dreams on Instagram and close deals in conversations. A reel of a beach brings "price for 2?" and "December dates?" comments by the hundred, and each one is a potential booking that needs a fast, specific reply.

This playbook covers how agencies automate the first reply and hand qualified leads to their travel consultants.

Qualify in one message

Ask for the three details a consultant needs: travel month, number of travellers and rough budget. With those, your team can send a proper quote instead of starting from scratch. Keep it to one message; a long form loses people.

A sample package DM

Package enquiry DM

Here's our {{destination}} package: {{package_link}}
{{nights}} nights, from {{starting_price}} per person, with {{inclusions}}.
Reply with your travel month and number of travellers, and our consultant will send a quote.

Itineraries as lead magnets

A sample itinerary is the most requested travel freebie. Deliver it after capturing an email, and follow up with a consultant's note offering to customise it. Date each itinerary and update it when hotels or routes change.

Payment reminders

Instagram only lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message, so reminders days later belong in email, WhatsApp or your booking system, not Instagram DMs. We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Send payment schedules and balance reminders by email or WhatsApp utility messages, with the traveller's consent.

Visa and documents

Share general document checklists, but avoid promising visa outcomes. Point travellers to official sources for current rules, and have a consultant handle specific cases.

Seasonal campaigns

  • Plan winter and summer holiday content two to three months ahead.
  • Run festive and long-weekend packages with a DATES keyword.
  • Prepare itineraries before posting the reel, not after the comments arrive.

Hand off to consultants

Export leads with month, group size and budget to your CRM daily. A consultant who calls with a tailored option the same day wins more bookings than one who replies with a generic brochure later.

Cancellation and change policies

Travellers ask about cancellations before they pay. A POLICY keyword that sends your cancellation and change terms, clearly and in plain language, builds trust and reduces disputes later. Make sure the automated reply matches the terms in your contracts.

Group tours and corporate trips

Group departures and company offsites bring high-value bookings. A GROUP keyword can share fixed departures and collect group size and dates, then route to a senior consultant.

Reviews and trip stories

Travellers who loved their trip are your best advertising. Invite them to tag you in their photos and share the best ones, with permission, as social proof on your account.

Measure

Track enquiries, itineraries sent, quotes and bookings by destination and by post. Destinations with many enquiries but few bookings may need clearer pricing in the first reply.

Frequently asked questions

How can travel agencies use Instagram automation?

To answer package enquiries instantly, send sample itineraries and capture traveller details for consultants.

What should a travel package DM include?

A package summary with inclusions and a starting price, and a request for travel month and group size.

Can I send payment reminders through Instagram?

Not reliably, because of the 24-hour window. Use email or WhatsApp with consent.

How fast should a travel agency reply to Instagram enquiries?

The first reply should be instant; a consultant's tailored quote the same day.

Should automation answer visa questions?

Only with general checklists and official sources; specific cases need a consultant.
