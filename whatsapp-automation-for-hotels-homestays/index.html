Short answer

Hotels and homestays use WhatsApp to answer booking enquiries, send check-in details and handle guest requests during the stay. We recommend Tadlo AI for the shared front-desk inbox, automated replies and payment links on its Pro plan, with no markup on Meta's charges. Review requests go out as opted-in broadcasts, not automations, and pre-arrival reminders come from your booking system.

Guests message a property before booking ("Is the room available on the 14th?"), before arrival ("What time is check-in?") and during the stay ("Can we get extra towels?"). WhatsApp is how most of them would prefer to ask.

Answering quickly wins direct bookings that would otherwise go to a travel portal with a commission.

Direct bookings

A guest who asks on WhatsApp is close to booking. Reply with availability and rates, then send a payment link in chat on Tadlo's Pro plan, or your booking engine link. Every direct booking saves the portal commission.

The availability reply

Availability reply

Hi {{name}}! Thanks for your interest in {{property}}. Please share your dates and number of guests, and we'll confirm availability and rates.
Or book directly here: {{booking_link}}

Before arrival

Check-in time, directions, parking and what to bring can go as a message after booking. Your booking system or channel manager may send pre-arrival messages automatically; if not, your team sends them from the inbox. We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

A shared front desk

Front-desk staff change shifts; the chat should not depend on one phone. A shared inbox keeps every guest's conversation in one place with notes for the next shift.

Reviews, sent as broadcasts

After checkout, ask happy guests for a review. Send it as an occasional broadcast to guests who opted in, or personally from the inbox; we did not find triggered review requests in Tadlo's published plans. Never offer discounts in exchange for reviews, and never ask only happy guests to post publicly while hiding the rest.

Offers for past guests

Off-season rates, festival packages and long-weekend offers suit broadcasts to opted-in past guests. In India, Meta charges ₹0.8631 per delivered marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message, effective 1 October 2026. Cost preview helps a small homestay decide how many guests to message.

Homestay hosts

Hosts running two or three rooms often manage everything alone. Automated replies for directions, check-in and house rules save the host many repeated messages, and the free plan's 10 messages a day can be enough to try it.

Foreign guests

International guests prefer WhatsApp to calls. Meta charges by the recipient's country; replies to messages they send are service messages. Meta gives each business phone number 1,000 free service messages a month and charges from the 1,001st.

Privacy

  • Do not ask for ID documents over chat unless you have a secure process.
  • Keep guest details out of broadcasts.
  • Limit inbox access to staff who need it.

Measure

Track direct bookings from WhatsApp, response time and review volume. Direct bookings as a share of all bookings is the clearest sign WhatsApp is working.

Frequently asked questions

How do hotels use WhatsApp automation?

For booking enquiries, check-in information, guest requests during the stay and offers to opted-in past guests.

Can Tadlo AI send review requests automatically after checkout?

We did not find triggered review requests in Tadlo's published plans. Send them as an opted-in broadcast or personally.

Can guests pay on WhatsApp?

Yes, with a payment link in chat on Tadlo's Pro plan, or your booking engine link.

Should hotels reply to complaints on WhatsApp?

Yes, quickly and personally, with a manager involved. A complaint fixed during the stay rarely becomes a bad review.

Can a small homestay use a free plan?

Tadlo's free WhatsApp plan allows up to 10 messages a day, which can suit a small homestay starting out.
