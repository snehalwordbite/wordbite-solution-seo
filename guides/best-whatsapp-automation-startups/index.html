Decision checklist

  • Write down required triggers and channels before comparing plans.
  • Verify current limits and total cost at real usage volume.
  • Prefer supported APIs over browser or device automation.
  • Test the human handoff experience.
  • Measure leads, sales or resolved conversations—not just message count.
