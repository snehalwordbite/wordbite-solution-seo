Short answer

A local DM funnel turns a post into a visit: a local offer with a keyword, an instant DM with the offer code, directions and hours, and a reason to come this week. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; it sends the offer by keyword in seconds and has unlimited DMs on every plan.

For a café, salon, gym or shop, success on Instagram is not a sale online; it is someone walking through the door. The DM is the bridge: it delivers the offer, the address and the reason to come now.

This guide lays out a simple funnel that local businesses can run without a marketing team.

The local funnel

  1. A post or reel with a local hook and a keyword: "Comment OFFER for this week's deal."
  2. An instant DM with the offer code, address, map link and hours.
  3. A deadline that is real: valid until Sunday.
  4. The customer shows the code in store.
  5. Staff record the code so you can count visits.

The DM that gets a visit

  • The offer and code in the first line.
  • Address, landmark and a map link.
  • Opening hours and the best time to come.
  • One line on parking or how to find you.

Make the code countable

Use a code that staff can see, such as INSTA-WEEK, and keep a tally at the counter. A few weeks of counts tell you which posts bring footfall, the number that matters most for a local business.

Local content that works

  • Behind the scenes on a busy morning.
  • New items or services, shot in your space.
  • Your team, so people know who they will meet.
  • Customer moments, shared with permission.

Answer the questions people ask before visiting

"Are you open today?", "Do I need to book?", "Is there parking?" Set DM keywords for HOURS, BOOK and DIRECTIONS so these get instant answers any time of day.

Stories for same-day traffic

A story at noon saying "quiet afternoon, reply TODAY for a walk-in deal" can fill slow hours. A story reply opens the 24-hour window, so you can answer follow-up questions too.

Seasonal offers

Tie offers to local moments: exam season for a café near a college, wedding season for a salon, new year resolutions for a gym. A timely offer with a keyword feels relevant rather than promotional.

Loyalty from Instagram

A customer who came in with an Instagram code is a good candidate for a return visit. Ask at the counter if they would like to hear about next month's offer on WhatsApp or by email, and keep the list for your next promotion. Regulars bring more steady revenue than one-off deals.

Neighbourhood partnerships

Team up with a nearby business for a joint offer, such as a café and a bookshop, and both post with the same keyword. Each DM sends both offers. It doubles your reach in the neighbourhood at no cost.

Keep it honest

  • Real deadlines only.
  • Honour the code even if a customer is a day late, when you can.
  • Do not promise availability you cannot check.

Measure

Track keyword DMs per post and codes redeemed per week. Posts with many DMs but few redemptions may need a stronger offer or clearer directions.

Frequently asked questions

How can a local business use Instagram DMs to get customers?

Post a local offer with a keyword, send the code, address and hours by DM instantly, and count codes redeemed in store.

What offers work for local Instagram marketing?

Simple, time-limited offers that bring people in: a free add-on, a trial class or early access.

How do I track walk-ins from Instagram?

Use a visible offer code and keep a tally at the counter.

How often should a local business post offers?

One clear offer a week is plenty for most local businesses; more often and offers stop feeling special.

Can automation answer "are you open today?"

Yes. A HOURS keyword in DMs can send your hours instantly.
