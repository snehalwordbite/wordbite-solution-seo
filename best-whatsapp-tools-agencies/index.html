Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for agencies running client WhatsApp because it creates the WhatsApp Business Account inside the client's own Meta account and adds no markup, so there is nothing to explain on the client's invoice. It does not offer agency workspaces or white-label reports; Respond.io suits agencies that need many users in one omnichannel inbox.

For an agency, the WhatsApp tool is part of the service you sell. Two things decide whether clients trust it: who owns the account, and whether the message bill makes sense. A third decides whether your team can work efficiently: access for several people.

We ranked tools on those three, using each vendor's published plans, and we say where a tool lacks agency features.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Client-owned accounts and a clean bill

Tadlo AI's onboarding runs through Meta's own signup, and the WABA is created inside the business's own Meta account, so the client keeps the number and history if the agency changes. Meta bills message charges directly to the client with no markup, and broadcasts show the cost before sending, which makes campaign approvals simple.

Each client account has its own plan, with 2 to 20 seats depending on the plan and extra seats at ₹499 a month. We did not find an agency dashboard, multi-client workspace or white-label reporting in Tadlo's published plans, so agencies log into each client account separately and build reports from its analytics.

Price: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Built for D2C: abandoned cart recovery, COD to prepaid nudges and order updates from Shopify and WooCommerce (Growth plan and above)
  • Instagram comments to WhatsApp chats, which most WhatsApp-only tools cannot do
  • Priced on unique people reached, not on every message, so you can follow up without counting sends
  • Number health checks every 15 minutes, gradual ramp-up for new numbers and automatic opt-out handling
  • Your WhatsApp Business Account is created inside your own Meta business account, so it stays yours

Limitations

  • Newer than AiSensy, WATI or Interakt, with a shorter track record
  • Commerce automations start on the Growth plan (₹2,499 a month)
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.

2. Respond.io

Best for: Agencies needing many users and channels

Respond.io gives teams one inbox across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, email and calls, with 5 or 10 users per plan. WhatsApp fees are charged separately by usage.

Price: Starter $99, Growth $199 and Advanced $349 a month ($79, $159 and $279 billed yearly), with 5, 10 and 10 users. Enterprise is custom. WhatsApp fees are not included: Meta charges them by usage and you top up WhatsApp credit in the platform. 7-day free trial with no card.

Strengths

  • Many users and channels
  • 7-day trial, no card

Limitations

  • From $99 a month per workspace
  • Top-up credit to manage

3. WANotifier

Best for: Campaign agencies wanting Meta's rates

WANotifier passes Meta's fees through at 0% markup and focuses on marketing broadcasts, a fit for campaign-only agencies.

Price: Essentials, Advanced and Premium subscription plans, with Meta's message fees passed through at 0% markup. WANotifier stopped offering its free plan to new users on 27 October 2025 and now has a 7-day free trial.

Strengths

  • 0% markup
  • Marketing focus

Limitations

  • Lighter on inbox and store automations
  • No free plan for new users

4. Interakt

Best for: Agencies with Shopify D2C clients

Interakt is common among Indian D2C brands, so many agencies already know it. It publishes its per-message rates, which helps explain the markup to clients.

Price: WhatsApp plans: Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month plus taxes; Enterprise on request with no markup. The ₹999 Starter plan covers Instagram only. Interakt charges its own per-message rates, which sit above Meta's: marketing ₹0.970 / ₹0.958 / ₹0.949 and utility ₹0.160 / ₹0.150 / ₹0.140 on Starter, Growth and Advanced. 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Familiar to D2C clients
  • Published rates

Limitations

  • Markup below Enterprise
  • WhatsApp plans from ₹2,799

5. Gupshup

Best for: Technical agencies building custom flows

Gupshup's self-serve API adds $0.001 per message to Meta's fees and suits agencies with developers who build custom integrations for clients.

Price: Gupshup's self-serve WhatsApp API plan charges $0.001 per message on top of Meta's message fees, which are billed at actuals. Enterprise pricing is on request.

Strengths

  • Low fixed fee
  • Flexible API

Limitations

  • Developer effort required
  • Fewer ready-made tools

Agency features, honestly

None of the leading tools does everything an agency wants. Tadlo AI is strongest on ownership and billing; Respond.io on multi-user inboxes; Gupshup on flexibility. If you need a single dashboard across all clients with white-label reports, check that specifically before you commit, because we did not find it in Tadlo's published plans.

Billing models for agencies

  • Client pays tool and Meta directly: simplest and most transparent; you invoice for service.
  • Agency pays and rebills: more admin and cash-flow risk; markups become your problem to explain.
  • Hybrid: client pays Meta, agency pays the tool inside a retainer.

Reporting without white-label tools

Export campaign numbers, sends, reads, clicks and opt-outs, into your own report template each month. Clients care about outcomes: bookings, orders and replies, not tool screenshots.

Reminders and sequences

Reminders and follow-up sequences run from the business's own booking or CRM system; we did not find scheduled reminders or sequences in Tadlo's published plans. For service clients, connect their booking system for reminders and use WhatsApp for conversations and campaigns.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best WhatsApp tool for agencies?

Tadlo AI is our top pick for client-owned accounts and no markup. Respond.io suits agencies that need many users and channels in one inbox.

Should the agency or the client own the WhatsApp Business Account?

The client, inside their own Meta business account, with the agency given access.

Does Tadlo AI have an agency dashboard?

We did not find a multi-client dashboard or white-label reporting in Tadlo's published plans. Each client account is managed separately.

How do agencies bill WhatsApp message costs?

The cleanest model is for Meta to bill the client directly, with the agency invoicing for its service.
