Short answer

Restaurants and cafés should automate menu requests, table booking links, offers and the everyday questions: hours, location and parking. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: keyword replies in comments, DMs and story replies, with unlimited DMs on every plan.

Food photos and reels bring the most enquiries of any local business category: "Menu?", "Is it veg?", "Do you take bookings for 8?". Most of these come in the evening, when your team is busiest.

Automation answers them instantly, so the people who are planning tonight's dinner choose you.

Menus that stay current

Menus change with seasons and prices. Host the menu as a page or PDF on your site and send that link, so you update it once and every old post stays correct. Mark vegetarian, vegan and common allergens clearly; those are among the most asked questions.

Table bookings

  1. Send the booking link from a BOOK keyword.
  2. If you take bookings by phone, send the number and best time to call.
  3. Confirm bookings through your reservation system.
  4. For large groups, route to the manager.

Offers that fill quiet times

  • Weekday lunch offers.
  • Early-evening specials.
  • Free dessert with a code from Instagram.
  • Student or office-group deals.

Stories for tonight

A story at 5 pm with tonight's special and "reply BOOK" catches people deciding where to eat. A story reply opens the 24-hour window, so staff can confirm a table in the same thread.

Delivery and takeaway

If you deliver through apps or your own site, an ORDER keyword can send those links, separate from table bookings, so diners find the right option fast.

Reviews

Happy diners are your best marketing. A small card on the table inviting them to tag you in a story brings their friends to your account; respond to tags with a thank-you.

Waitlists for busy nights

Popular places fill up on weekends. If you are fully booked, a BOOK reply can say so honestly and offer the next available slot or a waitlist through your reservation system. Telling people early saves them a wasted trip and keeps their goodwill for next time.

Questions diners ask most

  • Is there parking or valet?
  • Do you have outdoor seating?
  • Are children and pets welcome?
  • Do you have vegan or Jain options?
  • What time does the kitchen close?

Set a keyword or story highlight for each, so these are answered before anyone has to ask.

Catering and bulk orders

Office lunches and party catering are high-value. A CATERING keyword can send your catering menu, minimum order and a contact for quotes, and route the conversation to the manager.

Festivals and special menus

Festive menus and set meals drive bookings in advance. Prepare the special menu link and a BOOK keyword before announcing, and update automated replies the day the special ends.

Measure

Track menu requests, booking clicks and offer codes redeemed each week. Dishes that drive the most enquiries deserve more reels.

Common mistakes

  • An old menu image pinned for months.
  • "DM for menu" with replies the next day.
  • No mention of vegetarian or allergen information.

Frequently asked questions

How can restaurants use Instagram automation?

To send menus, booking links, offers and opening hours instantly when people comment or message.

Can I take table bookings through Instagram?

Yes. A BOOK keyword can send your reservation link, and staff confirm larger or special requests.

How do I keep my Instagram menu up to date?

Send a link to a menu page you update, rather than an image that goes out of date.

Do restaurants need a booking system for Instagram bookings?

It helps. A booking link lets people reserve without waiting; without one, send your phone number and best time to call.

Can I answer allergy questions automatically?

Share your allergen information link automatically, but have staff answer specific allergy questions personally.

What offers work for restaurants on Instagram?

Time-limited offers for quiet periods, with a code people show in store.
