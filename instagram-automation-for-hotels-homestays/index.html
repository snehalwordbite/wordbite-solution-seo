Short answer

Hotels and homestays should automate room and rate enquiries with a direct booking link, answer check-in, location and policy questions instantly, and invite guests to tag and review. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: keyword replies in comments, DMs and story replies, with unlimited DMs on every plan.

Travellers discover stays on Instagram and then ask the same things: rates for their dates, availability, pets, early check-in, how to get there. Answering fast can win a direct booking that would otherwise go through a booking site with a commission.

This playbook covers what to automate for hotels, resorts and homestays.

Direct bookings

Send your own booking engine's link rather than a booking site's, and mention any direct-booking benefit you offer, such as a free breakfast or flexible check-in. Even a small perk is a reason to book with you.

Dates and availability

Rates depend on dates, so ask for them in the first reply if your booking link does not show live rates. A short "Which dates and how many guests?" lets staff quote accurately and quickly.

Pre-arrival information

Instagram only lets a business message freely within 24 hours of the guest's last message, so pre-arrival details days before check-in belong in your booking confirmation email or WhatsApp, with consent. We did not find scheduled messages in Tadlo's published plans.

Reviews and tags

Guests who loved their stay are your best marketing. Invite them at checkout to tag you in a story, and reply to tags with thanks. If you ask for reviews, ask everyone, not only happy guests, and never offer rewards for positive reviews.

Events and group stays

  • A GROUP keyword for weddings, retreats and corporate stays.
  • Route to the sales manager quickly.
  • Share a package brochure after capturing an email.

Homestays: personal touch

Homestays sell personality. Keep automated replies warm and sign them with the host's name. Hand over to the host for anything about the house rules, food or local tips.

Measure

Track rate enquiries, booking-link clicks and direct bookings by month. Compare direct bookings from Instagram with bookings through commission-based sites.

Responding to complaints in public

When a guest complains in your comments, reply publicly with a short apology and an invitation to message you, then resolve it privately and quickly. Never automate replies to complaints. Future guests read how you handled problems more closely than they read your praise.

Local experiences

Guests often ask what to do nearby. A GUIDE keyword that sends your own short local guide, with restaurants, sights and day trips, is useful before booking and reminds people why your location is worth choosing.

Questions guests ask most

  • Is breakfast included?
  • Can I check in early or check out late?
  • Are pets or children allowed?
  • Is there parking?
  • How far is it from the station or airport?

Answer each with a keyword reply, and add them to a story highlight so guests can find them without asking.

Seasonality

Push rates and offers a few weeks before peak season and long weekends, with automations ready before the posts go live.

Frequently asked questions

How can hotels use Instagram automation?

To answer rate and availability enquiries with a direct booking link, and share check-in, location and policy details instantly.

Can I send pre-arrival messages on Instagram?

Not reliably, because of the 24-hour window. Use your booking confirmation email or WhatsApp with consent.

How do I get more direct bookings from Instagram?

Reply fast with your own booking link and a small direct-booking benefit.

Should I quote rates in Instagram DMs?

Send starting rates and your booking link with live rates. Exact quotes depend on dates and guests, which staff can confirm.

Can I ask guests for reviews on Instagram?

Invite all guests to tag or review you, and never reward positive reviews.
