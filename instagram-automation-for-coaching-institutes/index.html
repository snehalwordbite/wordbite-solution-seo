Coaching institutes market heavily on Instagram: toppers' stories, concept explainers, batch announcements. Admission season brings a flood of the same questions from students and parents: which batch, what fees, when does it start, is there a demo?

Answering each by hand is slow, and slow replies send families to the institute down the road. This playbook covers what to automate, what to keep personal, and how to measure admissions from Instagram.

Students and parents ask differently

Students ask about batches, faculty and results; parents ask about fees, safety, travel time and refund policies. Use separate keywords where you can, and write replies for the person most likely to send them. A parent who gets a clear fee breakdown in the first message is far more likely to visit.

Demo classes as the main conversion step

  1. Post a concept explainer with "Comment DEMO for a free class".
  2. The DM sends demo dates and a sign-up link, and asks for the student's class or exam.
  3. Capture the email or phone number for the confirmation.
  4. Confirm the demo by email or WhatsApp with the time and location.
  5. After the demo, a counsellor follows up personally.

Fee reminders: not on Instagram

Instagram only lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message, so reminders days later belong in email, WhatsApp or your booking system, not Instagram DMs. We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Use your fee management system or WhatsApp utility messages for instalment reminders, with the family's consent.

Admission season spikes

Result days and admission windows bring sudden comment spikes. A tool with unlimited DMs that queues sends within Instagram's limits makes sure every enquiry gets an answer, even when hundreds arrive in an hour.

Keep claims careful

  • Share past results honestly, with the year and context.
  • Do not promise ranks, marks or selections.
  • State fees, refund and transfer policies clearly.
  • Get consent before sharing any student's name or photo.

Hand off to counsellors

Automation answers the first question; counsellors close admissions. Route enquiries that ask about scholarships, special cases or transfers straight to a person, and export captured leads daily so counsellors can call while interest is high.

Scholarship and test announcements

Scholarship tests and results days drive the most traffic. Prepare a TEST keyword with dates, registration and syllabus before announcing, and keep a counsellor on the inbox for the following days.

Online and offline batches

Many institutes now run both. Ask which the student prefers in the first reply, and send the right schedule and fees. Mixing online and centre-based details in one message is a common source of confusion and wrong enrolments.

Content that brings enquiries

  • Short concept explainers with a DEMO keyword.
  • Batch announcements with dates and a BATCH keyword.
  • Faculty introductions.
  • Parent FAQs answered on camera.

Frequently asked questions

How can coaching institutes use Instagram automation?

To answer admissions enquiries instantly with batch details, fees and demo class links, and to capture student and parent contacts.

Can I send fee reminders through Instagram?

Not reliably, because Instagram limits messages after 24 hours. Use your fee system, email or WhatsApp with consent.

How do I get more demo class sign-ups from Instagram?

Post explainers with a DEMO keyword that sends dates and a sign-up link, then confirm by email or WhatsApp.

Should automation answer scholarship questions?

No. Route special cases like scholarships and transfers to a counsellor.
