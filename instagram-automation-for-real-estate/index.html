Short answer

Real estate teams should automate brochure delivery by comment keyword, instant replies to property enquiries with key details and a site visit link, and lead capture with budget and location. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: keyword DMs, email capture into a leads inbox and unlimited DMs on every plan.

Property reels and walkthroughs generate a lot of interest and a lot of repetitive questions: price, size, location, possession date. Every one is a lead worth capturing, and every slow reply sends the buyer to another listing.

This playbook covers what to automate on Instagram for developers, brokers and agents.

Qualifying questions

Ask one or two questions in the DM: budget range and preferred configuration, or whether they are buying to live or invest. It helps your sales team prioritise and makes the follow-up call more useful. Keep it short; long forms in DMs lose people.

A sample enquiry DM

Property enquiry DM

Thanks for asking about {{project_name}}! Here's the brochure: {{brochure_link}}
{{configurations}} from {{price_range}}.
Book a site visit: {{visit_link}}, or reply with your budget and we'll suggest options.

Site visits

  1. Send a booking link with available slots.
  2. Confirm by email or WhatsApp with the address and contact person.
  3. A person calls the day before for high-intent visits.
  4. Record the source post for every visit.

Brochures and accuracy

Keep brochures dated and accurate. Prices, availability and approvals change; an automated DM with outdated details damages trust. Update the link in the DM whenever the brochure changes.

Compliance

Include the registration and approval details your local rules require in brochures and listings, and avoid promising returns or appreciation in automated messages.

Hand leads to sales

Export leads with budget, configuration and source to your CRM daily. Speed matters: a property lead contacted the same day is far more likely to visit.

Content that drives enquiries

  • Walkthroughs with the keyword said on camera.
  • Neighbourhood guides.
  • Construction updates for under-construction projects.
  • Answers to common buyer questions.

Video enquiries

Buyers often ask for a video walkthrough before visiting. Keep a short walkthrough link for each project ready in your keyword replies, and offer a live video call through your team for serious buyers who cannot visit yet.

Brokers with many listings

Brokers often post several properties a week. Use a different keyword per listing, such as the project name or a short code, so each DM sends the right brochure and you can see which listings attract buyers. Keep a simple sheet of keywords and links, and retire them when a property is sold.

Rental enquiries

Rentals move faster than sales. For rental listings, the DM should send rent, deposit, availability date and a visit link, and ask when they want to move. Same-day replies matter even more here, because good rentals go within days.

Measure

Track enquiries, brochures sent, site visits and bookings per post. Posts that drive visits are worth boosting; posts with many comments and no visits may be attracting browsers rather than buyers.

Frequently asked questions

How can real estate agents use Instagram automation?

To send brochures by keyword, reply instantly to property enquiries, book site visits and capture leads with their budget and preferences.

What should a property enquiry DM include?

The brochure, configurations and price range, a site visit link and one qualifying question.

Can I book site visits through Instagram?

Yes. Send a booking link by keyword and confirm by email or WhatsApp.

Should I share prices in Instagram DMs?

A price range helps buyers decide whether to visit. Give exact pricing on a call or visit, where you can explain offers and charges.

How do I keep automated property details accurate?

Date brochures and update the DM link whenever prices, availability or approvals change.
