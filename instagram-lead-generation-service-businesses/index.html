Short answer

Turn Instagram into a lead source by posting content that invites a specific enquiry, answering every comment or DM instantly with a DM that asks one qualifying question, and sending a booking link or handing off to your team. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: instant comment and DM replies, lead capture into a leads inbox and unlimited DMs on every plan.

For a salon, clinic, studio or consultancy, an Instagram follower is not a lead until they ask something. The path from post to booking has four steps, and most businesses lose people between them because replies are slow or the next step is unclear.

This guide sets out the path and how to automate each step without losing the personal touch.

The comment-to-booking path

  1. A post or reel that ends with an invitation: "Comment PRICE for our menu" or "DM BOOK for a slot".
  2. An instant DM with the answer and one question.
  3. Lead details captured: name, email or phone, what they want.
  4. A booking link or a handoff to a person.
  5. A confirmation and, where relevant, a move to WhatsApp for reminders.

Ask one qualifying question

The first DM should answer and ask one thing that helps you serve them: which service, which location, which day. Long forms in DMs lose people; one question keeps the conversation going and opens Instagram's 24-hour messaging window when they reply.

Capture contact details

Ask for an email or phone number before sending detailed menus or brochures. Store each lead with the post it came from, so you can see which content produces bookings, not just likes.

Hand off at the right moment

Automation is great for the first answer and the link; people are better at reassurance and scheduling special requests. Route anything specific, such as a medical history question or a custom package, to a person.

Booking links

If you use an online booking system, send its link in the DM. If you book by phone or WhatsApp, send that instead. Either way, one clear next step beats a list of options.

Ads that feed the same path

If you run Instagram ads, point them at the same path: a click-to-Direct ad opens a DM where your automation answers and asks the qualifying question. Instagram lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message (up to 7 days after a click-to-Direct ad), and a person on your team can reply within a separate 7-day human agent window. That longer window after an ad click gives your team more time to follow up.

Reviews and referrals

After a good appointment, ask happy customers to tag you in a story or leave a review. Their posts reach friends nearby, which is the most valuable audience a service business can have.

Common mistakes

  • "DM for price" with replies hours later.
  • Asking for full details before answering anything.
  • No booking link, only "call us".
  • Not tracking which posts produce bookings.

Frequently asked questions

How do service businesses get leads from Instagram?

By posting content with a clear enquiry CTA, replying instantly with a DM that asks one question, capturing contact details and sending a booking link.

Should I put prices on Instagram?

A starting price helps people decide to ask. Send the full menu in the DM, with a booking link.

How fast should I reply to Instagram enquiries?

Instantly for the first answer, which automation handles, and within business hours for anything a person needs to answer.

How many questions should my DM ask?

One, maybe two. Each extra question loses some people before they book.

Can I track which posts bring bookings?

Yes. Capture leads with their source post, then match bookings to leads.
