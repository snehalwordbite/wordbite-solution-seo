Short answer

Offer a specific guide or brochure in a post, ask people to comment a keyword, and send it by DM after capturing their email. Then follow up by email with one useful next step. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job: keyword delivery, email capture into a leads inbox and unlimited DMs on every plan.

For service businesses, a lead magnet is often more useful than a discount: a price guide, a brochure, a checklist for choosing a provider. It attracts people who are researching, which is exactly when they choose who to call.

This guide covers what to offer and how to deliver it through Instagram.

Delivery setup

  1. Host the file as a link that opens on a phone.
  2. Post with a keyword: "Comment BROCHURE for the full PDF."
  3. The DM asks for an email, then sends the link.
  4. Save the lead with the post it came from.
  5. Send a follow-up email within a day.

Ask for the email honestly

Say what they will get and that you may follow up: "Share your email and I'll send the brochure, plus a note when new units open." People who know what to expect are better leads.

The follow-up email

  • Resend the lead magnet.
  • Offer one next step: a call, a visit or a booking.
  • Answer the most common question about it.
  • Sign it from a real person.

Keep lead magnets current

Prices, schedules and availability change. Date each brochure, update it when details change, and replace the link in your DM so old posts keep sending accurate information.

Regulated businesses

Clinics, financial advisors and similar businesses should keep lead magnets educational and have them reviewed by whoever handles compliance. If your business is in a regulated category, follow Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy and Commerce Policy, and keep medical, health and investment claims out of your messages.

Where the lead magnet lives

Host files somewhere stable, such as your own website, rather than a personal drive link that can break or ask for access. A stable link means old posts keep working, and you can update the file without changing every automation.

Formats that work for service businesses

  • A two-page PDF with prices or packages.
  • A short video tour or walkthrough.
  • A checklist for choosing a provider in your field.
  • A schedule or timetable with dates.

Choose the format your customers would actually save or share with someone they decide with.

Gated or open?

Asking for an email before the lead magnet captures more leads; sending it openly builds goodwill and reach. Many service businesses gate detailed brochures and price guides, and share general tips openly.

Measure

Track requests, emails captured and consultations booked per lead magnet. The best lead magnet is the one that produces bookings, not the one with the most downloads.

Common mistakes

  • A generic "company profile" nobody wants to read.
  • Asking for phone, email and address before sending anything.
  • No follow-up at all.
  • Links that break after you update the website.

Frequently asked questions

How do I deliver a brochure through Instagram?

Ask people to comment a keyword, and send the brochure link in a DM after capturing their email.

What makes a good lead magnet for a service business?

Something researchers want: a price guide, brochure, schedule or checklist for choosing a provider.

Should I ask for a phone number or email?

Email first. Ask for a phone number only if you will call, and say so.

How often should I update my brochure?

Whenever prices, packages or availability change, and at least once a quarter. Update the link in the DM at the same time.

How do I follow up after the lead magnet?

Send an email within a day with the file again and one clear next step.
