Short answer

Use a BOOK keyword in comments, stories and DMs that sends your booking link with a line on availability, ask one question if needed, and confirm the booking by email or WhatsApp after. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; it answers BOOK instantly in comments, DMs and story replies with the same rules.

Many customers would rather book from Instagram than call. The friction is in the back-and-forth: "any slots tomorrow?", "what about 5?", "how much?". A booking link sent instantly cuts most of that.

This guide covers the setup and the details that reduce no-shows.

The setup

  1. Put "DM BOOK to book" in your bio and on relevant posts.
  2. Create a keyword rule for BOOK and variants: book, booking, appointment, slot.
  3. The DM sends your booking link and one line on availability.
  4. Add a story highlight that explains how to book.
  5. Test from a second account.

A booking DM that works

Confirmations

Instagram limits messages to 24 hours after the customer's last message, so do not rely on Instagram for confirmations days later. Your booking system's email or a WhatsApp utility message is better for confirmations and day-before notes.

Reducing no-shows

  • Confirm the date, time and address in writing.
  • Ask for a deposit for long or high-value appointments.
  • Send a reminder through your booking system or WhatsApp, with an easy way to reschedule.
  • Keep a short cancellation policy in the confirmation.

Stories as a booking trigger

A story showing today's open slots with "reply BOOK" fills gaps fast. Because a story reply opens the 24-hour window, the DM can ask which slot and send the link.

Booking for several locations

Businesses with more than one branch should ask which location first, then send that branch's booking link. A short LOCATION keyword answer with each branch's address and link saves customers from booking at the wrong one, which is a common cause of no-shows.

Pricing in the booking DM

People hesitate to book when they do not know the price. Include a starting price or a link to your menu in the booking DM, so the booking page is the next step rather than another question. For services priced after a consultation, say so plainly and offer the consultation slot instead.

Group and repeat bookings

Group bookings, such as bridal parties or corporate sessions, need a person. Set a GROUP keyword that collects the date, number of people and contact details, and passes them to your team. For regular clients, a short message after each visit with the rebooking link keeps your calendar full without chasing.

Handling special requests

Route anything unusual, such as group bookings, medical questions, or custom packages, to a person. Automation should get the basics done and step aside.

Measure

Track BOOK requests, link clicks and completed bookings per week. If clicks are high but bookings low, look at the booking page itself: too many steps, or no slots at convenient times.

Frequently asked questions

Can customers book appointments through Instagram DMs?

Yes. A BOOK keyword can send your booking link instantly, and a person can confirm special requests.

How do I send booking confirmations from Instagram?

Use your booking system's email or a WhatsApp utility message, because Instagram limits messages after 24 hours.

How do I reduce no-shows from Instagram bookings?

Confirm in writing, send a reminder through your booking system or WhatsApp, and consider deposits for long appointments.

Should I take deposits through Instagram?

Take deposits through your booking system or a payment link, not by asking for card details in DMs.

Can a story fill last-minute slots?

Yes. Post today's slots with "reply BOOK", and the automation sends the link.
