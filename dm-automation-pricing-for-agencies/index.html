Short answer

Most agencies charge a one-time setup fee plus a monthly retainer for DM automation, priced on the work and outcomes, not on message volume. Pick a tool whose cost does not jump when a client's post goes viral; we recommend Tadlo AI because every plan has unlimited DMs. It has no agency dashboard, so allow time for managing accounts separately.

Pricing DM automation is tricky because the tool cost and the value can both swing. A client's reel can bring ten times the usual comments in a week. If your tool bills per contact or caps DMs, your margin or the client's results suffer exactly when things go well.

This guide covers pricing models and how tool choice affects them. We do not quote market rates, because they vary too much by city and client size.

Three pricing models

Models and when they fit
ModelHow it worksFits

Retainer plus performanceBase fee plus a share or bonus on leads or salesE-commerce and lead-gen clients with clear tracking

What drives your price

  • Number of automations and posts each month.
  • Whether you write the content CTAs too.
  • Reporting depth and review calls.
  • Industry risk: regulated clients need more care.
  • How many accounts and languages.

Tool costs and your margin

Tool pricing models behave very differently as a client grows. Per-contact pricing rises with every new commenter; monthly DM caps can stop sending on the best day; unlimited plans stay flat. A flat or free tool cost makes your retainer predictable and keeps conversations with clients about results, not overage bills.

Who pays for the tool?

The cleanest arrangement is for the client to hold and pay for the tool on their own account, with the agency fee covering the work. It keeps ownership clear and avoids you carrying costs if the client leaves.

Hidden time costs

Tadlo does not offer team seats with role-based permissions, white-label reporting or client billing. Tadlo's plans cover one Instagram account on Free and Creator and up to three on Pro. Budget time for logging into each client's account and building reports by hand. That time belongs in your retainer.

Performance pricing, carefully

Performance elements work only when tracking is reliable, such as tagged links and a CRM. Agree in writing what counts as a lead or sale and how it is measured, before the first month starts.

Writing the proposal

  • State the deliverables and the number of automations clearly.
  • Show the tool cost separately and who pays it.
  • Say what is out of scope, such as answering DMs by hand.
  • Set a notice period and what the client keeps if they leave.

Raising prices

Review pricing every six months. Clients who see more leads and bookings accept increases more easily when your reports show outcomes clearly.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I charge for Instagram DM automation?

Most agencies charge a setup fee plus a monthly retainer, priced on work and outcomes. Rates vary widely by market.

Should I include the tool cost in my retainer?

Usually it is cleaner for the client to pay for the tool on their own account.

Which tool keeps client costs predictable?

A plan without per-contact billing or DM caps. Tadlo AI has unlimited DMs on every plan.

Can I charge on performance?

Yes, if tracking is reliable and you agree in writing what counts as a lead or sale.
