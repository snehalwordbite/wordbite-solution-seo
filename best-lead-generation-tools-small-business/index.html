Short answer

Tadlo AI is our top pick for service businesses capturing leads on Instagram and WhatsApp: it captures emails and enquiries from comments and DMs with unlimited DMs, and gives your team a shared WhatsApp inbox with no markup on Meta's charges. ManyChat suits teams that need deep flows and integrations, and Respond.io suits teams handling many channels.

Service businesses, such as clinics, salons, coaching institutes, agencies and consultants, do not sell from a cart. They sell through conversations: an enquiry, a few questions, a booking or a call. Lead generation for them means capturing the enquiry quickly and getting a person into the conversation.

We ranked tools on capture, handoff and what they cost as enquiries grow. Where a tool lacks a feature in its published plans, we say so.

1. Tadlo AI

Our top pick

Best for: Capturing enquiries on Instagram and answering on WhatsApp

On Instagram, Tadlo AI answers comments and DMs instantly, asks for an email inside the DM, and stores every lead in a leads inbox with the post it came from. Its link-in-bio page captures emails from profile visitors too. Every Instagram plan has unlimited DMs.

On WhatsApp, a shared team inbox from the Starter plan at ₹999 a month lets your team pick up conversations, with automated replies for common questions. Meta bills message charges directly to your own account with no markup.

Be clear about one gap: we did not find scheduled follow-up sequences or appointment reminders in Tadlo's published WhatsApp plans. Follow-ups are handled by your team in the inbox.

Price: Instagram: Free forever plan with no card needed. Paid plans start at ₹249 a month in India or $5 a month elsewhere. Every plan, including Free, has unlimited automations and unlimited DMs (no monthly DM quota and no per-contact charge; sends are paced within Instagram's limits). WhatsApp: Free forever plan with up to 10 messages a day. Starter ₹999, Growth ₹2,499, Pro ₹5,999 and Scale ₹11,999 a month, priced on unique contacts engaged per month. Meta's message charges are billed by Meta directly to your own account, and Tadlo adds no markup. Prices exclude GST.

Strengths

  • Unlimited DMs and automations on every plan, including Free, so a viral reel never raises your bill
  • Email capture, comment-to-product carousels and a link-in-bio page in the same tool
  • No markup on Meta's message charges: Meta bills your own WhatsApp Business Account directly, and Tadlo only charges a flat plan
  • Your WhatsApp Business Account is created inside your own Meta business account, so it stays yours

Limitations

  • Facebook comment-to-DM and Messenger keyword replies are still being added
  • Tadlo AI is onboarding brands in stages, and you request access on its WhatsApp page.
  • We did not find scheduled WhatsApp follow-up sequences or reminders in its published plans
  • Tadlo does not offer team seats with role-based permissions, white-label reporting or client billing.

2. ManyChat

Best for: Deep qualification flows and integrations

ManyChat can run long qualification flows with tags, custom fields and integrations across Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and more. It bills by active contacts, which grows with your enquiries.

Price: ManyChat bills by active contacts. Free: 25 active contacts, any 2 channels, up to 4 active automations, 1 user and ManyChat branding. Essential: $17 a month ($14 billed annually), 250 active contacts, any 2 channels, unlimited custom automations, 2 users, no branding. Pro: $39 a month ($29 billed annually), 2,500 active contacts, any 3 channels, broadcasts and AI included, 3 users. Business: $99 a month ($69 billed annually), 7,500 active contacts, unlimited channels, 5 users, shared team inbox. Advanced: $199 a month ($139 billed annually), 25,000 active contacts, 10 users. Paid plans charge per active contact beyond those included. Essential and Pro have a 14-day free trial.

Strengths

  • Deep flows and integrations
  • Many channels

Limitations

  • Billed by active contacts
  • Free plan covers 25 active contacts

3. Respond.io

Best for: Teams handling many channels

Respond.io gives a team one inbox for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, email and calls, starting at $99 a month with 5 users. WhatsApp fees are charged by usage.

Price: Starter $99, Growth $199 and Advanced $349 a month ($79, $159 and $279 billed yearly), with 5, 10 and 10 users. Enterprise is custom. WhatsApp fees are not included: Meta charges them by usage and you top up WhatsApp credit in the platform. 7-day free trial with no card.

Strengths

  • Omnichannel inbox
  • 7-day trial, no card

Limitations

  • From $99 a month
  • Users capped per plan

4. Gallabox

Best for: Sales teams working leads on WhatsApp

Gallabox gives sales teams CRM-style tools inside WhatsApp, useful for high-value services. Plans start at ₹2,999 a month billed quarterly.

Price: Basic ₹2,999, Essential ₹6,999 and Advanced ₹16,999 a month, billed quarterly, with a 7-day free trial and no card needed. Meta's message charges appear on your Gallabox invoice with a markup; Gallabox does not publish the percentage.

Strengths

  • CRM features
  • 7-day trial

Limitations

  • Markup percentage not published
  • Billed quarterly

5. InstantDM

Best for: Capturing phone numbers on Instagram

InstantDM collects emails and phone numbers in Instagram DMs from its Legend Pro plan, useful when your team calls leads back.

Price: Free forever: 1 account and 500 DM automations a month, no card. Billed yearly: Legend Pro $9.99 a month (unlimited, up to 800 an hour), Trendsetter $24.99 (up to 2,000 an hour) and Trendsetter Pro $49.99 (no hourly cap). Multi-account plans: Multi Starter $4.44 per account a month (3 accounts, $160 a year) and Multi Pro $8.25 per account a month (10 accounts, $990 a year). Paid plans have a 7-day trial.

Strengths

  • Email and phone capture
  • Unlimited monthly DMs on paid plans

Limitations

  • Instagram only in its published plans
  • Billed yearly in dollars

Instagram for capture, WhatsApp for conversation

Instagram is where many service businesses get discovered, through reels and local content. But the conversation that leads to a booking usually happens on WhatsApp, where customers are comfortable asking about prices and slots. A setup that links the two saves leads from falling between apps.

Speed matters most

For service enquiries, the first business to reply often wins the booking. Automation does not win the sale for you, but it makes sure every enquiry gets an instant, useful first answer while your team gets to it.

Questions to ask before choosing

  • Does the tool capture contact details inside the conversation?
  • Can several team members work the inbox?
  • How does it bill as enquiries grow: by contact, by message or flat?
  • Does it support follow-ups the way your team works?

Frequently asked questions

What is the best lead generation tool for a service business?

Tadlo AI is our top pick for Instagram and WhatsApp lead capture, with unlimited Instagram DMs and a shared WhatsApp inbox with no markup.

Can I capture leads from Instagram comments?

Yes. A comment keyword can send a DM that asks for an email or phone number and saves it with the source post.

Do I need WhatsApp as well as Instagram?

Most service businesses benefit from both: Instagram for discovery, WhatsApp for the conversation that leads to a booking.

Can Tadlo AI send automatic follow-ups to leads?

We did not find scheduled follow-up sequences in Tadlo's published WhatsApp plans; follow-ups are handled by your team in the shared inbox.
