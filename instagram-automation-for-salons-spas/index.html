Short answer

Salons and spas should automate price menus, booking links and offer codes on Instagram, and use stories to fill same-day slots. Rebooking reminders belong in your booking system, email or WhatsApp. We recommend Tadlo AI for the Instagram side: keyword replies in comments, DMs and stories with unlimited DMs.

Salons and spas live on Instagram: transformations, new treatments, team work. Every before-and-after brings "price?" and "which stylist?" comments, and every quiet afternoon is an empty chair you could have filled.

This playbook covers the salon automation setup, from menus to last-minute slots.

Transformations that bring bookings

A before-and-after reel should end with a specific CTA: "Comment BOOK to get this with Priya." The DM sends the booking link with that stylist pre-selected if your booking system supports it, plus the price range for the service. People book faster when they know who and how much.

Filling same-day slots with stories

  1. Post a story at midday with today's open slots.
  2. Add "reply TODAY to grab one".
  3. The automation replies with the booking link and a short offer if you run one.
  4. Because a story reply opens the 24-hour window, staff can answer follow-up questions the same day.

Rebooking reminders

Instagram only lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message, so reminders days later belong in email, WhatsApp or your booking system, not Instagram DMs. We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Most salon booking systems can send rebooking reminders by SMS, email or WhatsApp after a set number of weeks.

Bridal and event bookings

Bridal and party bookings are high-value and personal. Use automation for the first reply, packages and a consultation link, then hand over to a senior stylist. Ask for the date early; it decides everything else.

Offers without discounting your brand

  • Add-ons rather than discounts: a free treatment with a booked service.
  • First-visit offers with a clear end date.
  • Off-peak offers to fill weekdays.
  • Referral codes for regulars who bring friends.

Gift vouchers

Gift vouchers sell well around festivals and birthdays. A GIFT keyword that sends your voucher options and how to buy them online or in the salon turns a comment into a sale, and brings a new client through the door later.

Questions clients ask most

  • Which stylist is best for my hair type?
  • How long does the treatment take?
  • Do I need a patch test?
  • What aftercare is needed?

Answer the factual ones automatically, and route personal suitability questions to a senior stylist.

Staff and stylist accounts

Stylists often have their own followings. If they post work from your salon, give them their own keyword that sends your booking link with them selected, so their followers become your clients and you can see whose posts bring bookings.

Product sales

Many salons sell hair and skin products. A PRODUCTS keyword can send a carousel of what you stock, with in-store pickup or delivery details.

Measure

Track bookings from Instagram by service and by stylist. Stylists whose posts bring the most bookings should get more airtime on your account.

Common mistakes

  • Prices hidden behind "DM for price" with slow replies.
  • Booking links that do not work on phones.
  • Offers with no end date.

Frequently asked questions

How can salons use Instagram automation?

To send price menus, booking links and offers instantly, and fill same-day slots with story replies.

Can I send rebooking reminders through Instagram?

Not reliably, because of Instagram's 24-hour window. Use your booking system's SMS, email or WhatsApp reminders.

How do I fill empty salon slots?

Post a story with today's slots and a reply keyword that sends the booking link.

How do I handle bridal enquiries on Instagram?

Automate the first reply with packages and a consultation link, ask the wedding date, then hand over to a senior stylist.

Should salons show prices on Instagram?

Yes, at least starting prices. Send the full menu by DM.
