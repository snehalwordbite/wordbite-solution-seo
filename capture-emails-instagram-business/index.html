Short answer

Capture emails inside Instagram DMs when someone asks for something, and on your link-in-bio page for profile visitors, then export them with their source to your CRM or email platform. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; it stores emails in a leads inbox with tags, segments and source tracking, with CSV export.

Instagram conversations disappear into the inbox. An email address turns an enquiry into a contact your team can follow up properly, from a CRM, with a record of what they asked for.

This guide covers where to capture emails, how to ask, and how to hand leads to your other systems cleanly.

How to ask

Tie the ask to something they want: "Share your email and we'll send the full price list." Say if you will follow up. Ask for one field at a time; a form in a DM loses people.

Consent

Rules vary by country. In the UK, for example, the ICO says you must not send marketing emails to individuals without specific consent, apart from a limited soft opt-in for your own previous customers. Record when and where each person gave their email, and include an unsubscribe link in every marketing email.

Keep context with each lead

  • The post or ad it came from.
  • What they asked for.
  • Tags for service, location or interest.
  • The date.

Handoff to your CRM

  1. Export leads to CSV on a schedule, daily or weekly.
  2. Map fields: email, name, source, tags.
  3. Import into your CRM or email platform.
  4. Assign follow-up to a person or a sequence in that tool.
  5. Mark leads as contacted so nobody is missed.

Lead magnets that earn the email

People give an email for something specific: a price list, a brochure, a checklist, a schedule. "Sign up for updates" earns far fewer. Match the offer to the post, so a reel about a treatment offers that treatment's price guide.

Segments that help your team

Tag each lead with what they asked about, such as the service, location or budget, at the moment of capture. When you export, your team can follow up with the right offer instead of a generic message, and you can see which services attract the most interest from Instagram.

When to ask for a phone number

Ask for a phone number only when a call is the natural next step, such as high-value services or consultations, and say why: "We'll call to confirm your consultation time." For everything else, email is enough and people give it more readily.

Follow up quickly

Service leads cool fast. Aim to reach new leads within a working day, while they remember asking. An email that references exactly what they asked for gets far more replies than a generic newsletter.

Common mistakes

  • Capturing emails and never exporting them.
  • Losing the source, so you cannot tell which posts work.
  • Adding leads to a newsletter without saying so.

Frequently asked questions

How do I collect emails from Instagram for my business?

Ask for the email inside the DM when someone requests information, and add email capture to your link-in-bio page.

Can I send Instagram leads to my CRM?

Yes. Export leads to CSV with their source and tags, then import them into your CRM or email platform.

Do I need consent to email Instagram leads?

Rules vary by country; in many places you need clear consent for marketing emails. Say what people are signing up for and offer an easy unsubscribe.

What is the best way to store Instagram leads?

In one place with their source and tags, exported regularly to your CRM or email platform.

How quickly should I follow up a lead from Instagram?

Within a working day, referencing what they asked for.
