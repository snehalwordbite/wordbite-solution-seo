Short answer

Real estate teams use WhatsApp to answer property enquiries in seconds, send brochures and floor plans, and book site visits through a shared inbox. We recommend Tadlo AI for the inbox, automated first replies and broadcasts with cost preview and no markup on Meta's charges. Site-visit reminders and follow-up sequences run from your CRM, not from Tadlo.

Property buyers ask a lot before they visit: price per square foot, possession date, loan tie-ups, parking. Most of those conversations already happen on WhatsApp, often on a sales executive's personal phone, where they are lost when the person leaves.

Moving them to a WhatsApp Business API number with a shared inbox keeps every lead with the company, and an automated first reply means nobody waits while the team is on a site.

The first reply

Property enquiry reply

Hi {{name}}, thanks for asking about {{project}}. It has {{configurations}} from {{starting_price}}, possession {{possession}}.
Reply 1 for the brochure, 2 for floor plans, or 3 to book a site visit.

Brochures and floor plans

Keep your brochure, floor plans and price sheet as small PDFs or images ready to send. Buyers forward them to family, so put the project name, RERA number and contact on every page. Send price sheets only with the date they apply from.

Booking site visits

When a buyer replies that they want to visit, a sales executive picks up the chat in the shared inbox and agrees a slot. Record it in your CRM. Visit reminders and next-day follow-ups run from your CRM or calendar; we did not find scheduled reminders or follow-up sequences in Tadlo's published plans.

One number, many executives

A shared inbox lets several executives answer from one company number, with chats assigned to the person handling that buyer. When someone leaves, the history stays. Tadlo's plans include 2 to 20 seats depending on the plan.

Launch broadcasts

New tower launches, price revisions and offer windows suit a broadcast to buyers who opted in. A broadcast tool that shows the cost before you send helps you judge whether a list of 5,000 past enquiries is worth messaging. In India, Meta charges ₹0.8631 per delivered marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message, effective 1 October 2026.

From portal and ad leads

  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat directly, and messages are free for up to 7 days in that window.
  • Portal leads can be messaged with an approved template if they agreed to WhatsApp contact.
  • Hoardings and site boards can show a WhatsApp link or QR code.

Opt-in and consent

Meta requires businesses to collect opt-in that clearly states the person is agreeing to receive messages from the business, and names the business. Portal leads who only gave a phone number have not opted in to marketing on WhatsApp; ask in your first conversation and record the answer.

What to keep out of automation

  • Promises about appreciation or rental returns.
  • Legal or title questions, which need your legal team.
  • Final prices and discounts, which a person should confirm in writing.

NRI buyers

Buyers abroad prefer WhatsApp to calls across time zones. Video walkthroughs, location pins and document checklists sent in chat help them shortlist before they fly in. Note that Meta charges by the recipient's country, so messages to buyers outside India are billed at that country's rate.

Instagram to WhatsApp

If your projects get attention on Instagram reels, Tadlo can open a WhatsApp chat from Instagram comments, on paid plans of both its products, so interested viewers land in the same inbox as your other leads.

Measure

Track enquiries, site visits booked and bookings by source. Most teams find a small number of sources produce most visits; spend there.

Frequently asked questions

How do real estate businesses use WhatsApp automation?

For instant replies to enquiries, sending brochures and floor plans, booking site visits through a shared inbox, and launch broadcasts to opted-in buyers.

Can Tadlo AI send site-visit reminders automatically?

We did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Send visit reminders from your CRM or calendar.

Can I message property portal leads on WhatsApp?

Only if they agreed to WhatsApp contact, and with an approved template outside a conversation they started.

Do Click-to-WhatsApp ads cost extra per message?

When someone messages you from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad, a free entry point window opens for up to 7 days, during which Meta does not charge for messages.
