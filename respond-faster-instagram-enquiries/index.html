Short answer

Reply faster by automating the answers to your most common questions, sending an instant acknowledgement for everything else, and routing specific enquiries to the right person during business hours. We recommend Tadlo AI for the job; keyword replies in comments and DMs go out in seconds, with unlimited DMs on every plan.

A slow reply on Instagram is a lost customer. People message several businesses at once and book with whoever answers first. Yet most small teams cannot watch the inbox all day.

The fix is not working harder; it is deciding which questions automation answers and which a person answers, and making both fast.

Find your repeat questions

  1. Read your last fifty DMs.
  2. Group them: price, hours, location, booking, availability, other.
  3. Count each group.
  4. Automate the top three or four.

An instant acknowledgement for the rest

For questions automation cannot answer, send an instant note that a person will reply, with your hours. People wait patiently when they know a reply is coming.

Route to the right person

  • Pricing exceptions and bulk orders to the owner or manager.
  • Clinical or technical questions to the qualified person.
  • Complaints to whoever can fix them.

Business hours

Set clear hours in your acknowledgement message and on your profile. Outside hours, automation answers the common questions; people expect a person only during hours.

The 24-hour window

Instagram lets a business message someone freely for 24 hours after their last message (up to 7 days after a click-to-Direct ad), and a person on your team can reply within a separate 7-day human agent window. Reply to every enquiry within that window, or you may have to wait for the customer to message again.

Comments are enquiries too

"Price?" under a post is an enquiry. Reply privately with the answer and publicly with a short note, so others see that you respond.

One owner for the inbox

When several people share the inbox, decide who owns it each day. Shared responsibility often means nobody replies. A named owner per shift, with automation covering the common questions, keeps response times predictable.

Saved replies for your team

Not every reply should be automated, but most can be prepared. Write saved replies for the questions your team answers by hand, such as cancellation policy, group bookings and payment options, so human replies are fast and consistent too.

Cover holidays and busy days

On holidays or fully booked days, update the acknowledgement message: "We're closed today; we'll reply tomorrow from 10." Customers forgive a wait they were told about; they do not forgive silence.

Keep automated answers current

Wrong hours or old prices in an automated reply are worse than a slow reply. Update keyword answers whenever details change, and review them monthly.

Frequently asked questions

How can I reply faster to Instagram messages?

Automate answers to your top questions, send an instant acknowledgement for the rest, and route specific enquiries to the right person.

What should I automate on Instagram first?

Price, hours, location and booking questions, which make up most enquiries for many businesses.

Is it OK to auto-reply to customers on Instagram?

Yes, for factual answers and acknowledgements. Keep personal and sensitive questions for people.

Should I reply to Instagram comments as well as DMs?

Yes. A question in the comments is an enquiry. Reply privately with the answer and publicly with a short note so others see you respond.

Will customers mind automated replies?

Not if the answer is useful and a person is easy to reach. Make that clear in every automated reply.

How long do I have to reply to an Instagram message?

You can message freely for 24 hours after the customer's last message; after that, wait for them to write again.
