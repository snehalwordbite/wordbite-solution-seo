Short answer

NGOs use WhatsApp to answer donor and volunteer questions, share donation and sign-up links, and send impact updates as broadcasts to supporters who opted in. We recommend Tadlo AI for the shared inbox and broadcasts with cost preview, which matters on a nonprofit budget, with no markup on Meta's charges. Event reminders and recurring donor updates are broadcasts you send, not scheduled automations.

Supporters are more likely to read a WhatsApp update than an email newsletter. For NGOs, that makes WhatsApp a strong channel for impact stories, volunteer calls and event details.

It also costs money per marketing message, so a nonprofit needs to choose its audience and frequency carefully.

A supporter reply

Supporter reply

Hi {{name}}, thank you for reaching out to {{ngo}}! Reply DONATE to give, VOLUNTEER for open roles, or ABOUT for our registration details and annual report.

Impact updates

Short updates with a photo and one specific result, such as "120 children got school kits this month", work well as broadcasts to opted-in donors. Send monthly or less. Use real photos with consent, and report impact accurately with dates.

Costs for a nonprofit

Impact updates and appeals are marketing messages. In India, Meta charges ₹0.8631 per delivered marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message, effective 1 October 2026. A broadcast tool that shows the cost before you send helps you decide whether to message everyone or just recent donors. Replies to supporters who message you are service messages. Meta gives each business phone number 1,000 free service messages a month and charges from the 1,001st.

Volunteers

Post specific volunteer roles with dates. When someone replies VOLUNTEER, send the sign-up form, and have a coordinator follow up personally in the shared inbox. Volunteer shift reminders come from your volunteer management tool or a broadcast you send to that day's volunteers; we did not find scheduled reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Events and drives

Fundraisers, walks and donation drives need clear details: date, place, what to bring. Send them as a broadcast to supporters who opted in, and a reminder broadcast the day before.

Appeals in emergencies

During a disaster appeal, many people message at once. Prepare the donation reply in advance and put extra staff on the inbox. New business portfolios start with a messaging limit of 250 people in a moving 24-hour period, which can rise to 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 and then unlimited as you send high-quality messages and use at least half of your limit in 7 days.

Consent and trust

  • Collect opt-in on donation and volunteer forms, naming your organisation.
  • Never add people to lists from phone numbers collected for other purposes.
  • Opted-out supporters are left out of Tadlo broadcasts automatically.

Fraud and impersonation

Fake accounts collect donations using NGO names. Publish your official WhatsApp number on your website and in every update, and remind supporters you will only share donation links from your official site.

Beneficiaries

If you message beneficiaries, protect their privacy: no names or photos in broadcasts without consent, and no sensitive details over chat.

Measure

Track donations and volunteer sign-ups from WhatsApp, opt-outs after broadcasts and cost per broadcast. Send less often if opt-outs rise.

Frequently asked questions

How do NGOs use WhatsApp automation?

For donation and volunteer replies, impact updates to opted-in donors, event details and a shared inbox for supporter questions.

Can Tadlo AI send donor updates automatically?

Donor updates are broadcasts you send to opted-in supporters; we did not find scheduled or recurring messages in Tadlo's published plans.

Do NGOs pay for WhatsApp messages?

Yes. Meta charges per delivered marketing and utility message by country; nonprofits pay the same rates.

How often should NGOs send WhatsApp updates?

Monthly or less for impact updates, with extra messages only for events and urgent appeals.
