Short answer

Event organisers use WhatsApp to send ticket links instantly, answer attendee questions through a shared inbox, and send updates to attendees who opted in. We recommend Tadlo AI for the inbox, automated replies and broadcasts with cost preview and no markup. Reminders come from your ticketing platform, or you send them as a broadcast; we did not find scheduled or triggered reminders in Tadlo's published plans.

Concerts, workshops, conferences and weddings all generate a flood of questions in the final week: entry time, parking, dress code, refunds. Most organisers answer them one by one, from a personal phone.

A WhatsApp Business API number with a shared inbox lets a team answer together, and a broadcast puts the same update in every attendee's chat at once.

Before tickets go on sale

  • Set up automated replies for TICKETS, VENUE and FAQ.
  • Prepare the ticket link, venue map and FAQ document.
  • Add a WhatsApp opt-in to your ticketing form, naming your company.

The ticket reply

Ticket reply

Hi {{name}}! Tickets for {{event}} on {{date}}: {{ticket_link}}.
Reply VENUE for directions and parking, or FAQ for entry rules and timings.

Answering attendee questions

In the final week, put two or three people on the shared inbox, each with a seat, and use saved replies for common questions. Assign refund and accessibility requests to the person who can decide them.

Updates to attendees

Venue changes, gate timings and weather updates go to opted-in ticket holders as broadcasts. Updates tied to a ticket fit utility templates; promoting your next event is marketing. In India, Meta charges ₹0.8631 per delivered marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message, effective 1 October 2026.

Reminders, honestly

Many ticketing platforms send their own reminders by email or WhatsApp. If yours does not, send a reminder broadcast to opted-in ticket holders the day before; it is a broadcast you send, not a scheduled automation in Tadlo.

Selling on WhatsApp

For small events sold directly, Tadlo's Pro plan lets your team send a payment link in chat. Larger events usually sell through a ticketing platform, with WhatsApp handling questions.

Weddings and private events

Wedding planners use WhatsApp to share schedules, venue pins and transport details with guests. Ask the couple to collect guests' consent before you add them to any list.

Promoting the next event

After the event, attendees who opted in to marketing can hear about the next one. Send a thank-you with photos and an early-bird link as an occasional broadcast. Opted-out attendees are left out of Tadlo broadcasts automatically.

Messaging limits for large events

New business portfolios start with a messaging limit of 250 people in a moving 24-hour period, which can rise to 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 and then unlimited as you send high-quality messages and use at least half of your limit in 7 days. Build up your number's limit well before a large event so updates reach everyone.

Measure

Track ticket-link clicks, questions handled and opt-outs after each broadcast. Use the most common questions to improve your event page.

Frequently asked questions

How do event organisers use WhatsApp automation?

For ticket links, venue and FAQ replies, updates to opted-in attendees and a shared inbox for questions.

Can Tadlo AI send event reminders automatically?

We did not find scheduled or triggered reminders in Tadlo's published plans. Use your ticketing platform's reminders or send a broadcast.

How many people can I message at once?

New business portfolios start with a messaging limit of 250 people in a moving 24-hour period, which can rise to 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 and then unlimited as you send high-quality messages and use at least half of your limit in 7 days.

Can I add wedding guests to a WhatsApp broadcast?

Only guests who agreed to receive messages, ideally collected by the couple.
